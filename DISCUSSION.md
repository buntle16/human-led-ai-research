# Discussion

This repository is an invitation to examine and improve a practical framework for human-led, AI-assisted research.

## How to participate

Useful contributions include:

- A concrete failure mode the principles do not address
- A counterexample where human-led scope control performs poorly
- A method for keeping an AI system on task without suppressing useful discoveries
- A way to test whether a person understands an AI-generated conclusion
- A proposed protocol, interface, or evaluation metric
- Evidence from research or real-world practice that supports or challenges the framework

## Suggested discussion topics

### 1. Scope control
How should an AI distinguish between:
- completing the requested analysis;
- reporting an important side finding; and
- changing the research question?

What should require explicit human approval?

### 2. Human understanding
What observable test could establish that a human understands an AI-assisted result well enough to make a decision?

Is being able to explain the result sufficient, or should the human also be able to predict how it behaves under changed assumptions?

### 3. Evidence and uncertainty
How should systems represent observed facts, verified findings, interpretations, hypotheses, unknowns, and contradictory evidence without overwhelming the user?

### 4. Expert knowledge
How can domain experience and tacit knowledge guide an AI analysis while remaining open to challenge and correction?

### 5. Evaluation
What experiments could compare this approach with a less constrained AI workflow?

Possible measures include task completion, error detection, reproducibility, time to a defensible decision, user comprehension, and frequency of unapproved scope drift.

## Discussion norms

1. Critique ideas, methods, and evidence—not contributors.
2. Prefer specific examples and testable claims.
3. State assumptions and uncertainty.
4. Distinguish personal experience from general evidence.
5. Do not post private datasets, credentials, personal information, or confidential material.
6. Disagreement is welcome; unsupported certainty is not.

## Open question

**How can we build AI systems that expand what a person can investigate without exceeding that person's ability to understand, evaluate, and direct the investigation?**
