# Contributing

Thoughtful disagreement is welcome. This is a public draft, and it improves through criticism.

## How to contribute

- **Open an issue** to report a failure mode, a counterexample, or a question.
- **Open a pull request** to propose a change to the text. Explain the reasoning, and where possible include an example, a counterexample, or a testable proposal.

See [DISCUSSION.md](DISCUSSION.md) for suggested topics and discussion norms.

## Ground rules

1. Critique ideas, methods, and evidence, not people.
2. Prefer specific examples and testable claims.
3. Distinguish personal experience from general evidence.
4. Do not post private datasets, credentials, personal information, or confidential material.
